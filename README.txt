For use in MapTool.

Installation instructions:
1) Drag and drop poi-token-addon.mtlib into your campaign in MapTool
2) Drag and drop PoI.rptok into your campaign - note, reversing the order will cause MapTool to freeze, as it does not have the required assets yet!
3) The token is ready to use; clicking any button will change its image into the one displayed.
